- The repaired scenes could move the balance or sync of their neighbours. → The picture audits run on both staged editions before any render, and each repair is checked against the whole reverification list of its note.

- The engine's release could assemble a soundtrack that differs from release 1.0.1's. → The sound stage ran on the new release before any picture work: both soundtracks, timelines, captions and transcripts hold release 1.0.1's bytes.
