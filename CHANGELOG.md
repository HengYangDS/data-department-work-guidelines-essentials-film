# Changelog

All notable changes to the DDWG essentials films and their build are recorded here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog 1.1.0](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and releases follow [Semantic Versioning 2.0.0](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The Chinese and English essentials films of the Data Department Work Guidelines at `v8.0.0`, each exactly 600 seconds at 60 fps: what the guidelines are for and why they hold before how to follow them, told through one made-up incident, price data that arrived two hours late.
- The Mise tasks that rebuild both editions from the tracked sources with the published filmkit 0.14.0 release: `film:supply` installs the pinned browser build and speech models, `film:build` narrates, assembles, renders, packages and verifies both editions offline, and `film:compare` compares a rebuild with a published release.
- Each edition as two pictures, one with its captions drawn in and one without, and a player whose captions control switches between them while keeping the time and the play state.
- ETHOS governance with its portable proof.
- The MIT License for the code and CC BY 4.0 for the films' content, with the third-party notices and the narrator's provenance in `NOTICE`.
